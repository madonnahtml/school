Nuova pagina

Modello per una nuova pagina in materie/<materia>/, da registrare poi in assets/js/site.js:

<!doctype html>
<html lang="it" data-subject="informatica">
<head>
  <meta charset="utf-8">
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">
  <title>Titolo · Scuola</title>
  <link rel="stylesheet" href="../../assets/css/style.css">
  <script src="../../assets/js/site.js"></script>
  <script src="../../assets/js/layout.js" defer></script>
  <script src="../../assets/js/search.js" defer></script>
</head>
<body>
<main id="content">
  <h1>Titolo</h1>
  <h2>Prima sezione</h2>
  …
</main>
</body>
</html>
